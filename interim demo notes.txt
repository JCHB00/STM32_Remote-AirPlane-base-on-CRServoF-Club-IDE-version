1. Your progress: How much you have accomplished.

We are building an RC plane controlled by a stm32

In terms of progress, we have used a breadboard to do some testing as well as assembly of some of the circuitry, we have written most of code for the plane, including the data parsing, crsf controlling, and some failsafe protocols. (Show control of the servo with controller) After the receiver gets the eLRS signals, the chip decodes it and sends to the mapped servos and motor for control. Other than that, we build some failsafe for some expected failures that might happen, such as running out of battery midflight, (unplug LiPo battery) sorry for the loud noise, we have a buzzer that will scream if you run out of battery so you can know beforehand or you can have a better chance of finding the plane after it unfortunately crashes.
